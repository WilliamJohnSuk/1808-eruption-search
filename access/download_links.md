# Where to download the material flagged in the review

Compiled 16 Oct 2026 for the 1808/09 eruption search. Numbers follow the "Next archival targets" list in the evidence review doc.

**How to use this list**
- **Internet Archive (archive.org):** every item has a details page. The "DOWNLOAD OPTIONS" box on that page offers PDF and JP2/JPG page images. A direct PDF is usually at `https://archive.org/download/<id>/<id>.pdf`.
- **HathiTrust:** the full-view status shown below was checked from the catalogue. Full PDF download needs a member-institution login; outside the US, volumes printed after about 1896 may show as limited.
- **Link status:** "Checked" means I opened the page or catalogue record today. "Not opened" means it is the repository's standard entry point, which I did not open.
- **Saving scans for me:** put files in ~/Downloads and I can read the page images from there.

## Added 21 Oct 2026 (eruption-markers thread)

- **Roche, *Recherches sur les offuscations du soleil et les météores cosmiques* (1868).** A historical catalogue of times when the sun was dimmed, from antiquity to the 1860s. Its OCR is too garbled to search for "1808" or "1809", so it has to be read as page images. Details page: https://archive.org/details/bub_gb_5ypFAAAAcAAJ . Whole PDF (free, no borrowing needed): https://archive.org/download/bub_gb_5ypFAAAAcAAJ/bub_gb_5ypFAAAAcAAJ.pdf . **Wanted:** the whole book is short, so please save the full PDF into `~/Downloads/1808-9 Volcano Project/`. If it is long, the pages that matter are the chronological list of dimmings for 1780–1868 (the 1783 dry fog is mentioned on scan pages 15, 20, 26, 40, 66 and 68, so the list is probably in that stretch) plus the table of contents. **Read 22 Oct 2026 from William's download (page image: table of contents pp. v-viii, pp. 57-60): negative.** The catalogue has 32 numbered entries from 242 BC to 1867, with nothing between 1777 and 1860, so it has no 1808-1810 entry. The dry-fog chapter (pp. 57-60) cites only 1783, St Vincent ash on Barbados in 1812, the Quebec darkness of 16 Oct 1785, the Canadian darkness of 2-4 Jul 1814 (after Chladni's Catalogue, Annalen 1826), and quake-linked dim suns in 1802, 1804 and 1813. Pages saved as roche_pages/ in his volcano folder.
