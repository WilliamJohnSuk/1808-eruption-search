# Digitized newspapers for the 1808/09 eruption search: how to get at them

Compiled 16 Oct 2026. Window: Sept 1808 to mid 1809 (extend to late 1809 for papers that printed overseas news months late).

**How to read the status tags**
- **Opened here**: I loaded the page or file from this project's environment on 16 Oct 2026 and the details shown were checked.
- **Blocked here**: the site refused automated access (robots rule, bot wall or error). The details come from the repository's own user guide or catalogue and should work in a normal browser.
- **Catalogue only**: known from a catalogue, guide or earlier notes; I did not reach the collection itself.

Saving files for me: put downloads in `~/Downloads` (already approved) and tell me which; I can read page images from there.

## Open these first

1. **Gazeta do Rio de Janeiro 1808–09** at the Hemeroteca Digital Brasileira. This is the only full run online. Correction to `download_links.md`: the Brasiliana USP PDF holds a single 1817 supplement, not the 1808–21 run (see `notes/lusophone.md` l. 98).
2. **El Aviso (Havana) Oct 1808–Jun 1809** at the BNE Hemeroteca Digital. Every issue has a PDF and the text is OCR-searchable.
3. **Minerva Peruana (Lima) Dec 1808–Mar 1809** at the BNP Perú Biblioteca Digital, one PDF per issue. This is the Lima paper closest to Unanue's "mid-December" sighting.
4. **Gazeta de México and Diario de México, Dec 1808–Mar 1809** at the Hemeroteca Nacional Digital de México. The *Diario* is a daily, and it can be searched by date and by full text.

## 1. Latin America (Spanish and Portuguese)

### Gazeta do Rio de Janeiro (Rio de Janeiro), 10 Sept 1808 onward, twice weekly
- **Hemeroteca Digital Brasileira (BN Brasil)**. Blocked here.
  - Path: go to https://memoria.bn.gov.br, open the **Periódico** tab, type "Gazeta do Rio de Janeiro", **click the title in the drop-down** (the guide says typing it is not enough), choose period 1800–1809, then press Pesquisar. The direct viewer is https://memoria.bn.gov.br/DocReader/DocReader.aspx?bib=749664.
  - Account: none.
  - Download: there are two routes. For a page image, set the zoom to 100%, right-click the image and choose "Save image as" (up to 300 dpi). For a whole issue as a PDF, click the **calendar icon** in the left menu and then click the issue.
  - OCR: yes. Use "Busca no título em exibição" to search inside the title. A blue year folder contains hits, a green issue folder contains hits, and the number in brackets is the count of pages with hits. Accents are ignored. Suggested terms: *nevoeiro, névoa, cerração, vulcão, volcão, terremoto, cinza, sol, cometa, meteoro*.
  - Quirks: the viewer is a heavy Flash-era interface, and a desktop browser works best. The source for this method is the BN tutorial (https://www.gov.br/bn/pt-br/atuacao/colecoes-e-servicos-aos-leitores/publicacoes-seriadas/tutorial-basico_hdb_v5.pdf), which I opened here.
- **Internet Archive**, Sept–Dec 1808 only. Opened here.
  - Items: https://archive.org/details/gazeta-do-rio-de-janeiro-1808 (PDF 17 MB, JP2 zip 43 MB, OCR text), plus gazeta_rj_1808_003, _004, _005 and the extras gazeta_rj_extra_1808_002 and gazeta-rj-extra-1808-001-1. The 1809 single issues are `gazetadelriojane00unkn` (12 Aug 1809), `gazetadelriojane00unkn_0` (9 Aug), `gazetaextraordin00unkn_1` (28 Aug) and `extractodelagaze00unkn_1` (3 Jun).
  - Download: at https://archive.org/download/<id>/.
- **Brasiliana USP**: https://digital.bbm.usp.br/handle/bbm/8426. Opened here. The record is labelled "1808–1821" but the PDF (9 MB) is one 1817 *Gazeta Extraordinária*, so it is not useful for the window.

### Correio Braziliense (London, monthly, 1808–22; Portuguese-language)
- **Internet Archive** Google scans, `correiobrazilie00unkngoog` to `06`. Catalogue only (from the earlier pass). The volume-to-year mapping is uncertain, so check the title page of each.
- Also on the Hemeroteca Digital Brasileira; use the same path as for the *Gazeta*. Blocked here.

### El Aviso, papel periódico de la Havana (Havana), 27 Oct 1805 – 20 Feb 1810
- **BNE Hemeroteca Digital**. Blocked here.
  - Path: https://hemerotecadigital.bne.es/hd/es/issn/2219-7982 (the title card; the ISSN link was confirmed by the University of West Florida Havana source list, which I opened here). On the card, click **Calendario** to see issues by date or **Ejemplares** for a list, then tick 1808 or 1809.
  - Account: none.
  - Download: "Descargar" in the issue list saves the whole issue as a PDF. The viewer's download icon exports the issue or selected pages as PDF, JPG or **TXT (OCR)**.
  - OCR: full text. Use quotation marks for phrases and `|` for OR, for example `volcan | volcán | bolcan`. Ticking "páginas" returns only pages that contain all the terms. Wildcards `*` and `?` work but not at the start of a term. Highlighting on the page image works only in Firefox.
  - Licence: public-domain images are CC BY 4.0; credit "Imágenes procedentes de los fondos de la Biblioteca Nacional de España". The source for the method is the BNE short guide (https://www.bne.es/sites/default/files/repositorio-archivos/guia_abreviada_hd_0.pdf), which I opened here.

### Aurora, correo político-económico de La Habana
- **Prensa Histórica (BVPH), Colección iberoamericana**: https://prensahistorica.mcu.es/latinoamerica/en/publicaciones/listar_numeros.do. Opened here.
  - The 1808 listing shows Aurora no. 507 (31 Aug 1808) and 14 Dec 1808, plus *Gaceta de Caracas* t. I nos. 1–17 (24 Oct – 30 Dec 1808; no. 16 is missing). Each issue has "Digital copy" and "PDF" links.
  - Account: none.
  - OCR: "Search full text" box, filterable by year and month.
- Other Havana titles of the window are not online as far as I found. *Papel Periódico* stops in 1804, and *Diario de la Habana* starts on 10 Oct 1810.

### Gaceta de Caracas (Caracas), from 24 Oct 1808
- **Prensa Histórica**: as above, 1808 issues, with an issue PDF for each. Opened here (listing).
- **Internet Archive** facsimile, t. I (1808–10): https://archive.org/details/gacetadecaracas0001vari. The PDF is at https://archive.org/download/gacetadecaracas0001vari/. Catalogue only; it was full-text searched in pass 2 with nothing found.

### Semanario del Nuevo Reyno de Granada (Bogotá), 1808–10
- **Internet Archive**, John Carter Brown copy. Opened here (pass 1).
  - 1809 volume: https://archive.org/details/semanariodelnuev02unse, PDF at https://archive.org/download/semanariodelnuev02unse/semanariodelnuev02unse.pdf. Caldas's "Noticia" (No. 5, 5 Feb 1809) is at scan leaves 63–67, and the rain tables are at leaves 231–233.
  - 1808 volume: https://archive.org/details/semanariodelnuev01unse.
  - OCR: the "Search inside" box on the details page.
- **Biblioteca Digital de Bogotá** (No. 6): https://www.bibliotecadigitaldebogota.gov.co/resources/2090216/. Catalogue only.

### Redactor Americano and El Alternativo del Redactor Americano (Bogotá, 1806–09)
- This Bogotá paper ran right through the window and was not in the register before. Catalogue only.
- **Universidad Distrital repository** holds single-page TIFFs. For example, No. 33 (4 Mar 1808) is at https://repository.udistrital.edu.co/items/2db75d4f-691f-49f6-85be-d4296c8e907b (opened here). The collection is http://hdl.handle.net/11349/34194, where you should search for "Redactor Americano" with dates from Dec 1808.
  - Licence: open access, CC BY-NC-ND. Note that the items are clippings, not full issues.
- **Biblioteca Nacional de Colombia, Colección Digital**: the full issues are probably here. Search catalogoenlinea.bibliotecanacional.gov.co for "Redactor americano". Blocked here earlier.

### Minerva Peruana (Lima), 1805–10
- **BNP Perú Biblioteca Digital**. Blocked here (robots).
  - Path: open https://bibliotecadigital.bnp.gob.pe and search "Minerva peruana". Results are single issues, and you can sort or filter by date. Known 1808 items:
    - 25 Oct: https://bibliotecadigital.bnp.gob.pe/items/077be426-4904-4812-9771-fac566560f5e
    - 9 Nov: …/items/248e4657-e8f3-43b3-9569-7b883a1bd3c4
    - 26 Nov: …/items/8cba96eb-da64-4be2-8bd3-0651fd00d97c
  - Download: the PDF button on each item page.
  - Account: none for public-domain items.
  - OCR: not confirmed. Expect image-only PDFs.
- **Notre Dame (Durand collection)**: https://marble.nd.edu/item/001664311, dated "18–1810". Opened here. It has an "Open in external viewer" image link, but the page does not say which issues it holds. The 1810 run is at https://marble.nd.edu/item/92ea4ea3-04ba-48e3-be9e-f761061cde06.
- **Memoria de Madrid** lists 16 issues from 1808–10: http://www.memoriademadrid.es/buscador.php?accion=VerFicha&id=9569. Catalogue only.

### Gazeta de México (t. XV 1808, t. XVI 1809) and Diario de México (daily, 1805–17)
- **Hemeroteca Nacional Digital de México (UNAM)**: https://hndm.iib.unam.mx. Opened here (home page only).
  - Path: Búsqueda básica → **Por título** (letter G or D) at https://hndm.iib.unam.mx/consulta/, or go to **Búsqueda avanzada** at https://hndm.iib.unam.mx/consulta/busqueda and enter a date range (dd-mm-aaaa, for example 01-12-1808 to 31-03-1809).
  - Account: none for viewing.
  - OCR: the "Buscar en: contenido (texto completo)" option in Búsqueda avanzada.
  - Download: the user guide describes only viewing, plus a paid "Solicitar reproducción" service. Whether a PDF button now appears on 19th-century titles is not confirmed, and the guide dates from 2015. If there is no download, browser screenshots are acceptable for my reading.
  - Quirks: a closed padlock means the item can be viewed only inside UNAM, which should not apply to 1808 titles. The source is the HNDM user guide (https://hndm.iib.unam.mx/files/guia-de-usuario/guia-usuario-hndm.pdf), which I opened here.
- **HathiTrust or Google Books** scans of the *Gazeta de México*: search https://catalog.hathitrust.org for "Gazeta de Mexico". Blocked here. These are full view if public domain, but a PDF of the whole volume needs a member-institution login.

### Gazeta de Guatemala (vols. XII–XIII, 1808–09)
- **UCA El Salvador repository**: https://repositorio.uca.edu.sv. Search "Gazeta de Guatemala" and filter the dates to 1808–09. Blocked here. The 1810 issues are confirmed, for example https://repositorio.uca.edu.sv/entities/publication/51c5fb13-b24c-44bb-a39a-0159b7d77156, with a PDF per issue. The 1808–09 holdings are not confirmed.
- A survey article, "La prensa temprana en la era digital: contexto y recursos" (*Ístmica*, https://www.revistas.una.ac.cr/index.php/istmica/article/view/9900), lists Central American repositories. It was blocked here by a captcha.

### Gazeta de Puerto-Rico
- The earliest surviving digitized issue I found is from 1810 (IA `gazetadepuertori00brom`), so there is nothing for the window.

### Río de la Plata and Chile
- Buenos Aires had no newspaper between the *Semanario de Agricultura* (ended 1807) and the *Correo de Comercio* and *Gazeta* of 1810, and Chile had no press until 1812. The only route there is manuscript diaries such as Beruti's.

## 2. Spain and Portugal

### Gazeta de Madrid; Gazeta Ministerial de Sevilla (1 Jun 1808 – 10 Jan 1809); Gazeta del Gobierno (Junta Central, 6 Jan – 30 Dec 1809)
- **BOE historical collection**: https://www.boe.es/buscar/gazeta.php. Opened here.
  - The Seville patriot gazettes are in the same collection as the Madrid *Gazeta* (they are under "Denominaciones"), so they do not need the BNE as the register assumed.
  - Path: set "Fecha de publicación" to a date range and pick the title under "Denominaciones Gazeta".
  - Download: there is a PDF of the page images per item. The direct pattern `https://www.boe.es/gazeta/dias/YYYY/MM/DD/pdfs/GMD-YYYY-NNN.pdf` worked in the earlier pass, but a day-index page timed out today.
  - Account: none.
  - OCR: **text search only covers 1836–1959**. For 1808–09 you have to browse by date, although each PDF has an OCR text layer.
- The *Gazeta del Gobierno* is the patriot government's paper and would be the one to reprint American news reaching Cádiz or Seville in spring 1809.

### Diario de Madrid, Semanario Patriótico, Diario Mercantil de Cádiz, Correo Mercantil de España y sus Indias
- **BNE Hemeroteca Digital**: https://hemerotecadigital.bne.es → "Títulos", or a full-text search with the year filter set to 1808–1809. Blocked here.
  - Access is the same as for *El Aviso*: no account, an issue PDF per issue and OCR search.
  - I could not confirm which of these titles cover the window. Check each title card.

### Diario de Barcelona (1792–)

### Diario de Mallorca (Palma), daily with a weather table
- **Internet Archive**: https://archive.org/details/DiarioDeMallorca, one item per day, for example https://archive.org/details/DiarioDeMallorca_18081217. Opened here (pass 2).

### Gazeta de Lisboa (official, 1808–09)

  - **Hemeroteca Digital de Lisboa**: https://hemerotecadigital.cm-lisboa.pt. It offers PDFs, but its 1808 holdings are not confirmed; its "Invasões Francesas" programme (https://hemerotecadigital.cm-lisboa.pt/EFEMERIDES/InvasoesF/Invasoes.pdf) may list them.

## 4. What I could not do from here
- Every major newspaper viewer refused automated access from this environment: BN Brasil, BNE, Prensa Histórica search, BNP Perú, BN Portugal, HathiTrust, Gallica, Delpher, Trove and UCA. The Internet Archive item pages could be read as text, but the page images could not be opened.
- None of this affects a normal browser. The paths above come from each repository's own guide where one existed.
- In practice, you open and download the scans, and I read them from `~/Downloads`.

## 5. Paid aggregators and large free portals (added 16 Oct, for William's question)

**Why there is no single aggregator for Latin America:** only about a dozen newspapers were being printed in all of Spanish and Portuguese America in 1808–09, and the free national libraries above already hold most of them. Large volume for this window comes from the British, US and European press. Those papers reprinted letters and ship reports from Lima, Buenos Aires, Rio, Havana, the Cape and the Indies, usually two to four months late.

| Service | 1808–09 coverage that matters | Cost / access | OCR search | Le Moyne access | How we'd use it |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **British Newspaper Archive** (britishnewspaperarchive.co.uk; the same data is on Findmypast) | London dailies (*Morning Chronicle*, *Morning Post*, *The Sun*, *The Star*, *Morning Herald*, *Examiner* from 1808), many provincial and port papers. About 1 million pages from 1720–1880 were made free in 2021, including the *Morning Chronicle*, *Sun*, *Star* and *Examiner* | individual subscription, quoted as £80/yr in 2021; searching is free and shows snippets | yes | not listed (unconfirmed) | **best bet for volume.** You search and download page PDFs, and I read them |

**Large free portals not yet tried** (all free, all with OCR search):
- ANNO (anno.onb.ac.at): the Austrian National Library, including the *Wiener Zeitung* 1808–09.
- Deutsches Zeitungsportal (deutsche-digitale-bibliothek.de/newspaper) and ZEFYS (Berlin State Library).
- Gallica/RetroNews.
- Delpher. Its search interface already works from William's Mac.
- Svenska dagstidningar (tidningar.kb.se): *Post- och Inrikes Tidningar*.
- Danish Mediestream: pre-1920 material is free.
- Finland's digi.kansalliskirjasto.fi: Swedish-language papers.
- Europeana Newspapers: aggregates several of these.
- Papers Past (New Zealand) starts in 1839, so it is no use here.

**Can Claude search these directly?** For the subscription sites, no. I can't log in with your credentials, and these sites block the cloud environment. You run the searches, save the page PDFs or downloads into `~/Downloads/1808-newspapers/`, and I read them. For the free portals that have an API, the download script can do the work from your Mac, as it already does for Delpher.

**Other ways in without Le Moyne:** these were not checked and are only suggestions to verify:
- a free New York Public Library card for NY State residents (it gives remote access to some Gale and Readex databases)
- walk-in access at Syracuse University Libraries

**Syracuse University note:** the public database page (resources.library.lemoyne.edu/databases) loads its A–Z list with JavaScript, so I could see only the featured databases and "New York Times 1985–current (Gale)". To confirm, open that page, filter by **Newspapers** and check the list. You can also ask at ask.library.lemoyne.edu.

**Syracuse University Libraries (checked 16 Oct from its research guides and policy pages):**
- **Relevant for 1808–09:**
  - America's Historical Newspapers (Readex)
  - Caribbean Newspapers 1718–1876 (Readex)
  - Eighteenth Century Journals (c.1685–1815)
- **Not useful:** Latin American Newspapers 2 starts in 1822.
- **Not seen in the guides:** The Times, British Library Newspapers and BNA. The full A–Z list was not checked.
- **TDM Studio:** a pilot open to SU affiliates only, and its newspapers are mostly from 1990 onward. There was no sign of Gale Digital Scholar Lab.
- **Visitor access:** the licensed-resources policy says "Anyone using a library workstation may access these resources on site". The visitor page, however, says databases that need an SU NetID are not open to visitors. Confirm with libref@syr.edu before going. Bird Library is open to the public Mon–Fri 8am–7pm.
