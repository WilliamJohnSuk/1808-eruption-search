# Ship logs and journals covering 1 Mar – 31 Aug 1808, Bay of Bengal to the South China Sea

Compiled 16 Oct 2026 as a supplement to `/mnt/project-files/1808-eruption/catalogue_sweep_1808-09.md`. Every row is a record seen in a live catalogue. No row repeats a ship and log already listed in the sweep. Where a TNA piece already appears in the sweep under a *different* ship or log, the row says so ("piece in sweep for X").

**Catalogues used**
- **TNA**: TNA Discovery API, searched by ship name and series (ADM 51, ADM 52) with a 1807–1808 date filter. Piece contents were read from the search descriptions or the `API/records/v1/details` record.
- **TNA-OTH**: the TNA Discovery index of other repositories (held-by = other).
- **BL**: British Library archives catalogue (searcharchives.bl.uk), IOR/L/MAR/B.
- **UKHO**: UK Hydrographic Office, as indexed in Discovery. These are remark-book and survey pages, not logs, but they are dated observations.

**Positions** come from Wikipedia ship articles and the catalogue descriptions, and the column names the source. Anything marked **[inferred]** is not sourced.

**BL dates** run from the day the ship was taken up in the Thames, often 2–3 months before she sailed. Sailing dates in the position column come from Wikipedia.

**Digitised**
- "no (checked)" means the Discovery details API returned digitised = false.
- ADM 51/52 pieces I did not check individually are marked "no [series not digitised; unchecked]".
- BL records with an empty "Digitised content" field are marked "unknown".
