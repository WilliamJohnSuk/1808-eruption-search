# Catalogue sweep: diaries, scientific registers and ship logs covering 1808–09

Compiled 16 Oct 2026 for William's request to search WorldCat. **WorldCat's search pages refuse automated access from this environment** (robots block; individual `search.worldcat.org/oclc/<n>` record pages sometimes load). The sweep therefore used the catalogues that did respond, and each row names its catalogue: UK National Archives Discovery API (TNA records plus its index of other repositories), British Library archives catalogue (searcharchives.bl.uk), PANGAEA, SOAS, Royal Museums Greenwich, Wellcome, Europeana, archive.org, Memoria Chilena, Cervantes Virtual, Mystic Seaport, Providence Public Library, Nantucket Historical Association, SLNSW. Also blocked: ArchiveGrid, loc.gov, Pacific Manuscripts Bureau, NLA Trove, NLNZ, Biblioteca Digital Hispánica, AM Digital.

How to read it: every row is a record actually seen in a catalogue or database result. Ship positions or observer locations not stated in the record are marked *inferred*. Digitization: "No (checked)" = the catalogue says not digitised; "Not flagged / unknown" = not checked. Spot-checked against the live catalogue: BL IOR/L/MAR/B/173C (Wexford) and PANGAEA 872369 (Recife register).

Parts: **1** Royal Navy logs (TNA ADM 51/52/101); **2** East India Company, whaling and merchant logs; **3** diaries, travel journals and instrumental registers (this part also repeats a few RN logs from Part 1).

Best leads at a glance:
- Recife daily temperature/pressure register, Jan 1808–Dec 1810, A. B. Pereira do Lago (PANGAEA 872369, digitized data; original in Revista do IHGB).
- EIC Wexford (Cape 9 Dec 1808) and Preston (central/S Indian Ocean Dec 1808) journals, BL; Wexford is digitized on Qatar Digital Library.
- HMS Foudroyant and Bedford logs (Sidney Smith's squadron, Rio/River Plate) and HMS Cornwallis (Celebes/Moluccas Jan–Mar 1809), TNA, not digitized. [Corrected 28 Oct 2026: those actions were Jan–Mar 1810; in the window she was on the Arabian Sea side of the station; register B72.]
- LMS missionary journal, Tahiti, 10 Nov 1808–6 Jan 1809, SOAS.
- Whaler Cyrus (S Pacific, Jul 1808–Jul 1810) and Hero (S Atlantic/Indian Ocean, Nov 1808–Feb 1810), Nantucket Historical Association, digitized on archive.org.
- New Granada rain records to Dec 1808 (Popayán, Alegría, Cartagena) from Caldas's correspondents.

## Notes
- **Not found or excluded:**
  - Discovery has no separate ADM 51 entries for Piémontaise or Dédaigneuse covering late 1808. The only Dédaigneuse log ends 9 May 1808 (ADM 51/1911).
  - Powerful's logs end Feb 1808. Harrier's end 30 Nov 1808 (ADM 51/4454). Greyhound has a gap.
  - Marlborough (ADM 51/1979, 3 Aug 1808 – 31 Aug 1809) is excluded because she was probably back in home waters.
  - Diana (ADM 51/2418, 29 Oct 1808 – 28 Jul 1809) is excluded because Wikipedia gives her no South America service.
  - Steady, Pitt and Monarch were not searched.
- **ADM 101:** Discovery's ADM 101 coverage for 1808–09 is thin. Only Albion (above) fits the target regions. Searches for Culloden, Foudroyant, Bedford, Agamemnon, Elizabeth, Confiance and Raisonable, and by keyword (Brazil, Rio de Janeiro, Cape of Good Hope), returned nothing relevant. The West Indies has Argo (ADM 101/86/3D, 22 Jul 1808 – 21 Sep 1809, Surgeon Robert Purdie, C11541465) if needed.
- **ADM 55** (explorers' logs) was not searched.
