# Hosted page images

Page images of historical records that the project cites, hosted only where we believe the record is in the public domain or openly licensed. Only the cited pages are hosted, not whole volumes.

Every image has a line in [`index.csv`](index.csv): file, holding archive, item reference, image or page, date of the record, licence or public-domain basis, attribution line, and the finding or register row that cites it. These images are not covered by the repository's CC BY 4.0 licence; each keeps the status stated in its row.

**What is here (28 Nov 2026).** Items the 4 Nov 2026 licence audit marked HOST with the licence read:

- `semanario_nrg_1809_no05_banrep1606/`: *Semanario del Nuevo Reyno de Granada* no. 5, 5 Feb 1809 (Caldas, F01).
- `boe_gazeta_madrid/`: 15 single pages of the *Gazeta de Madrid*, 1809-10, each the page that carries a volcano or earthquake item the reading passes cite (all negative for the 1808/09 veil). Cut unchanged from the BOE PDFs, with the OCR text layer kept.
- `papers_cc_by/`: the cited pages of two open-access papers (Plunkett et al. 2023; Nardin et al. 2020), with each paper's licence page, and our crop of Nardin Table 2.

Two cleared datasets are in `data/`, not here, because they are tables rather than page images: the Recife register (PANGAEA 872369) and an extract of the American Offshore Whaling Voyages database. Their licence lines are in `data/README.md`.

Library of Congress newspaper pages are cleared by the audit, but the project does not hold or cite any LoC newspaper page for 1808-10 at present, so none is hosted. Items the audit marked HOST-AFTER-CHECK (for example Kagoshima, Nationaal Archief, Google-digitised Internet Archive scans, British Library and Qatar Digital Library images, Georgia Historic Newspapers) wait for their terms check.

Never hosted here: FamilySearch images, purchased record copies (for example TNA page checks), pages from in-copyright books or paywalled papers, and anything an archive's terms forbid. Those are linked to the archive instead (see `strands/method/hosting_licence_audit_2026-11-04.md` and `data/hosting_licence_audit_2026-11-04.csv`).

**Takedown.** If you hold rights in, or believe we should not host, any item here, email 1808@williamsuk.ai and it will be taken down promptly while we look into it.
