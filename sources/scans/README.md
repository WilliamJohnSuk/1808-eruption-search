# Hosted page images

Page images of historical records that the project cites, hosted only where we believe the record is in the public domain or openly licensed. Only the cited pages are hosted, not whole volumes.

Every image has a line in [`index.csv`](index.csv): file, holding archive, item reference, image or page, date of the record, licence or public-domain basis, attribution line, and the finding or register row that cites it. These images are not covered by the repository's CC BY 4.0 licence; each keeps the status stated in its row.

Never hosted here: FamilySearch images, purchased record copies (for example TNA page checks), pages from in-copyright books or paywalled papers, and anything an archive's terms forbid. Those are linked to the archive instead (see `strands/method/hosting_licence_audit_2026-11-04.md` and `data/hosting_licence_audit_2026-11-04.csv`).

**Takedown.** If you hold rights in, or believe we should not host, any item here, email widzodev@icloud.com and it will be taken down promptly while we look into it.
