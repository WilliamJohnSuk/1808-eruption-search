**Question.** Do daily journals in North America record a veil, and was there a Northern Hemisphere eruption in the window that could add to the ice signal?

**What was read.** Hudson's Bay Company post journals of York Factory, Churchill, Moose Factory and Edmonton House (page images); interior and north-western journals; US diaries; eruption catalogues for Kamchatka, the Aleutians, Japan and Iceland.

**Main results**
- **F09** [page image]: "The darkest day I have seen in Hudsons Bay", York Factory, 4 Nov 1808. Probably weather, and a month before Bogotá.
- **F10** [OCR]: dark days and a very red sun at Salem, 8-12 Nov 1808; probably smoke, and too early.
- We found no haze, dry fog, coloured sun or odd twilight in the HBC journals read; the writers rarely record such things, so the negative is weak.
- No North Pacific or Icelandic eruption with a firm date in 1808-1810 was found. Candidates for a small Northern Hemisphere contribution are poorly dated (Sheveluch, Klyuchevskoy "1810", Zaō late 1809, Tarumae).
