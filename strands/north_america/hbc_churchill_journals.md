## Direct answer on 4 Nov 1808
**There is no corroboration at Churchill.** The 4 Nov 1808 entry (B.42/a/134, PDF p6R, read at 400 dpi; the date is in a run of individually dated entries) records only trade and hunting returns. 1–3 Nov and 7–10 Nov are lumped as "Men employed as the days before / as before". The only weather word between 26 Oct and 14 Nov is "Snowing" on 12 Nov. **This does not refute York's "darkest day".** The Churchill writer recorded little weather and nothing about the sky, and he condensed whole weeks and months. A dark day that did not stop work would very probably not have been entered. Churchill is about 200 km NW of York.
