## 0. Bottom line

1. **No independent dated sky observation from New Granada or Quito (Dec 1808 – mid 1809) was found** in anything reachable from here. Caldas's printed Noticia (Semanario Año 2, No. 5, 5 Feb 1809) remains the only source.
2. **Concrete new lead: Caldas's letters to Santiago Arroyo in Popayán dated 21 Jan, 6 Feb and 6 Mar 1809** (also 6 Jun and 21 Aug 1809).
   - These letters were written while the veil was current. Arroyo, with Antonio Arboleda, was Caldas's Popayán observer.
   - They are printed in Posada (ed.), *Cartas de Caldas* (Bogotá 1917), in the ACCEFYN *Cartas de Caldas* (1978) and in *Cartas de Caldas ilustradas* (2016).
   - The 1917 edition is digitized at Banco de la República, but that host is robots-blocked from here. **Their text was not reached.**
   - **No Caldas letter is known between 6 Nov 1808 and 21 Jan 1809.**
3. **No daily meteorological register by Caldas covers Dec 1808 – Jan 1809.**
   - His printed Bogotá series covers Jan–Jun 1808.
   - He then "interrumpió sus observaciones hasta el año 1810" (Sociedad Geográfica de Colombia, *Reseña histórica del Observatorio*).
   - The Popayán, Alegría and Cartagena rain tables end in Dec 1808. These are the originals Caldas said he kept ("que mantenemos originales", Semanario leaf 231, about No. 25–26, Jun 1809).
4. **Volcano and earthquake catalogues show no regional eruption in 1808–09:**
   - **Galeras:** Cepeda 2020 (SGC) has no documented activity between the 1796–c. 1802 interval and 1823.
   - **Puracé:** Pulgarín et al. 1993 (INGEOMINAS) lists 1789, then 1816.
   - **Cotopaxi:** Wolf 1878 has no 1808 or 1809 entry. The last episode is 4 Jan 1803.
   - **Ecuador seismicity:** Beauval et al. 2010 has no 1808–09 event of 10 or more intensity points. Its only event between 1790 and 1830 is Riobamba 1797.
   - **Colombia seismicity:** the SGC and SOGEOCOL summaries show no 1800–1815 entries. The only local tremors in the window remain J. M. Caballero's Bogotá quakes of 7 and 15 Jan 1809 (from pass 1). These are minor and unrelated to the veil.
5. **Semanario 1809, further FTS** (archive.org `semanariodelnuev02unse`):
   - **No second meteorological note** and no follow-up report from Pasto, Popayán, Neiva, Santa Marta or Tunja.
   - "temblor" gave 0 hits.
   - "volcan", "nube", "Tunja" and "Neyva" hits fall only in the Noticia (leaves 63–65) or in unrelated Humboldt and Restrepo texts.

## 2. Caldas's observers' original sheets ("que mantenemos originales")

- **Semanario 1809, leaf 231** (pass 2): "Estos jovenes físicos nos han remitido los pormenores de sus observaciones, que mantenemos originales … solo hay comunes desde Agosto hasta Diciembre de 1808."
  - The observers were Rodríguez Torices (Cartagena), Arboleda and Pérez Valencia (Popayán), and Campo Larraondo (Alegría).
  - Nieto, Castaño & Ojeda (2005, *Revista de Indias* 65(235):683–708, https://revistadeindias.revistas.csic.es/index.php/revistadeindias/article/download/386/455/872) cite Semanario **núm. 26, 2 Jul 1809, pp. 152–154** for Mosquera's Popayán population report. Semanario leaves 233–235 hold the Popayán vital statistics, which places the rain article in **No. 25 or 26 (late Jun to 2 Jul 1809), printed pp. ~145–152**. This is an inference.
- **Fate of the sheets:**
  - **Not traced.** No catalogue reached here lists them.
  - Candidate repositories:
    - **Real Jardín Botánico, Madrid, Archivo de la Real Expedición Botánica (Div. III).** The Observatorio was part of the Expedición. About 100 boxes were shipped from Santafé with an inventory dated 22 Jul 1816 and reached Cádiz in 1817. Source: Burbano Trimiño 2023, ICANH, as reviewed in *RGID* 34(2), 2024, p. 413, https://revistas.ucm.es/index.php/RGID/article/download/99588/4564456571471/4564456731327. Caldas's observatory papers may be in this shipment, but that is **unconfirmed**.
    - Casa Museo Caldas (Bogotá).
    - Biblioteca Nacional de Colombia, manuscripts.
    - Archivo Central del Cauca (Popayán).
    - Vanderbilt MSS.0950, "Francisco José de Caldas Map Collection": maps plus a series "Field Notes by Caldas", c. 1802–1810. https://collections.library.vanderbilt.edu/repositories/2/resources/1967. Digitized items 52408 and 52410 are field notes and a Magdalena level graph. They do not appear to be meteorological, and are not dated 1808–09 per the catalogue.
- **Caldas's own register:** Jan–Jun 1808 was published in the Semanario (1849 ed.; PANGAEA doi:10.1594/PANGAEA.872046). SOGEOCOL, "Reseña histórica del Observatorio Astronómico" (https://sogeocol.edu.co/documentos/004_01_rese_hist_obser_astr.pdf, p. 2), says: "Caldas interrumpió sus observaciones hasta el año 1810, año en que se publicaron las hechas en el mes de enero." **So no instrumental Bogotá series exists for Dec 1808 – Feb 1809.** This is a secondary statement, but consistent with pass 1, citing Mejía: "se interrumpieron en 1809".
