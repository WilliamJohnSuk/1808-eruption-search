## 3. New finds, not on Commons

### 8. Lithograph signed "A. Maurin", frontispiece of Mendoza 1909 (best new find)

- **What:** bust portrait captioned "FRANCISCO JOSÉ DE CALDAS", signed lower left "A. Maurin" [page image]. Antoine Maurin (1793-1860) was a Paris lithographer [background, unchecked]. Facing the title page of Diego Mendoza, *Expedición botánica de José Celestino Mutis al Nuevo Reino de Granada y Memorias inéditas de Francisco José de Caldas*, Madrid: V. Suárez, 1909.
- **Same type as no. 1:** the pose, coat, cravat and hair match the Espinosa lithograph and the PPI woodcut, so Maurin redrew the Espinosa likeness [inference, from side-by-side thumbnails]. When and for what Maurin made it is not known. Our guess is the 1849 Paris edition of Caldas's *Semanario* edited by Joaquín Acosta [inference, unchecked]; see U249.
- **Copies:** Internet Archive `expedicionbotani00mend` (University of California Libraries, 500 ppi), page n9, **1895 × 3518 px**: https://archive.org/details/expedicionbotani00mend/page/n9 ; file https://archive.org/download/expedicionbotani00mend/page/n9.jpg. Wellcome copy b24861340, canvas 12, 1972 × 3530 px: https://iiif.wellcomecollection.org/image/b24861340_0012.jp2/full/full/0/default.jpg (Wellcome marks this digitised book "in copyright", so use the Internet Archive copy).
- **Rights:** the artist died in 1860 and the book is from 1909; public domain in the US and in countries with life + 70 or 80 years. Commons already hosts the whole book as a PDF.
- **Host:** yes. Credit "Antoine Maurin, lithograph, in D. Mendoza, *Expedición botánica…* (Madrid 1909); scan University of California Libraries via Internet Archive".

### 9. Oil portrait, Universidad del Rosario, Bogotá

- **What:** half-length, slightly turned, white cravat, dark brown double-breasted coat; right hand holds dividers on a zodiac globe, under which the white *beca* (sash) of the Colegio del Rosario is visible; left hand on a notebook; long cartouche below [text]. Anonymous, 19th century, oil on canvas, 82 × 65 cm (framed 92.3 × 75.5 cm), Sala de Juntas. Caldas wore the Rosario *beca* from 1788.
- **Copy:** https://urosario.edu.co/museo/coleccion/francisco-jose-de-caldas ; image file https://urosario.edu.co/sites/default/files/2023-02/caldas-alter-2.jpg, **952 × 1200 px**.
- **Rights:** the painting is public domain by age. The page gives no licence for the photograph [text]. Under the US rule that a faithful photo of a 2-D public-domain work adds no new copyright (the rule Commons uses), it can be hosted; Colombian law on this point we have not checked.
- **Host:** probably yes, with credit to the Museo de la Universidad del Rosario and a link back. This is a project decision because the holder states no licence.

### 10. Ivory miniature, Casa Museo del 20 de Julio (possibly made in his lifetime)
