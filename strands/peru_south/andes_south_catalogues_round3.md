## 1. Findings

### Bottom line
None of the seven catalogues searched lists an eruption, ash fall, "volcanic" haze or eruption-like event in Peru, Bolivia, Chile, Argentina or southern Ecuador for 1806–1810. Sapper 1917 has no entry for Tonga, Vanuatu, New Zealand or the South Sandwich Islands in 1806–1810 either. The only events recorded are ordinary earthquakes. Note that **1809 is completely empty** in Polo (Peru), Goll (Chile) and Perrey 1858 (Peru, Colombia, Amazon). That fits a quiet regional record, and it also fits a gap caused by the independence crisis (1809–1810). These catalogues are mostly derived from one another (Polo, Perrey and Goll all draw on Unanue, the Lima almanacs and Mallet), so their silences are not independent.

### 1.1 Sapper, *Katalog der geschichtlichen Vulkanausbrüche* (Strassburg 1917)
- URL: https://archive.org/details/KatalogDerGeschichtlichenVulkanausbrueche. Full-text search was run for 1806, 1807, 1808, 1809, 1810, Antuco, Misti, Rauchwolke and Sandwich.
- The section on the Andes (Peru, Bolivia, Chile), pp. 296–311, has nothing for 1806–1810. Misti, p. 299: only "explosive Tätigkeit für 1784; doch dürfte sie nicht langdauernd und stark gewesen sein". Antuco: 1752 (p. 305), then 1828 and 1861 (p. 311). **Sapper omits the Villarrica and Antuco 1806 eruptions that GVP lists.**
- The only Southern Hemisphere hit outside Indonesia is **p. 90**: "[Nicht weit von 2° 43' SBr. und 22° 55' WL. wurde am 19. Mai 1806 eine Rauchwolke bemerkt⁷).]" ("Not far from 2°43'S, 22°55'W a smoke cloud was noticed on 19 May 1806"). This is the equatorial mid-Atlantic "submarine volcano" sea-report area. It is a bracketed (doubtful) shipboard report, 2.5 years too early, and cannot be a VEI 6 source. Footnote 7 was not read.
- Other dates in the window are all Northern Hemisphere or Indonesia: Vesuvius 1806, 1809 and 1810; Etna 27 Mar 1809; São Jorge (Azores) May–June 1808; São Miguel 1810; Taal 1808; Lamongan 1806–44; Krakatau 1807; Colima 1808–18; Unalaska 1806; Kamchatka early 1807.

### 1.2 Goll, F., *Die Erdbeben Chiles … Erdbeben und Vulkanausbrüche in Chile bis zum Ende des Jahres 1879* (München 1904)
- URL: https://archive.org/details/dieerdbebenchil00dessgoog. This is a chronological catalogue of earthquakes **and eruptions** in Chile, including the Peruvian south as far as Arica and Tacna.
- **The chronological list (pp. 18–19) jumps from "1796: Copiapó E. Mallet" straight to "1810, 14. Novbr. 11ʰ p. in Arica ziemlich st. E., 15 s D."** (1810, 14 Nov, 11 p.m., fairly strong earthquake at Arica, duration 15 s). There are no entries for 1797–1809, and no eruptions until "1820 Antuco grosse Eruption".
- Villarrica entries are 1640, 1852, c. 1860 and 1874–77. The earliest 19th-century Antuco entries are 1820 and 1827 (p. 105).
- **False hit:** the "1808" match on p. 131 is the 23 July 1898 Concepción earthquake (red sky and "fireballs"; telephones and electric light are mentioned; source *Globus* 1898). The year is an OCR or reference artefact, not 1808.

### 1.4 Polo, J. T., "Sinopsis de temblores y volcanes del Perú", *Boletín de la Sociedad Geográfica de Lima* t. 8 (1898), pp. ~355–378, continued in t. 9 (1899) for 1861–1898
- URL: https://archive.org/details/boletndelasocied8189soci. This is the standard Peruvian earthquake and volcano chronology.
- **p. 376 (1806):** a run of Lima earthquakes, mostly from Unanue's tables. Among them: "El 1.° de diciembre, de 6 á 6 y ½ de la tarde, fuerte temblor en Lima, que duró de 1 y ½ á 2 minutos" (the 1 Dec 1806 Lima/Callao earthquake); "El 16 de diciembre fuerte lluvia en la mañana en Lima, y temblor á las 6"; "El 30, á las 12 y tres cuartos del día, temblor algo fuerte en Lima." The exact year assignment of each p. 376 line could not be confirmed from the snippets.
- **p. 377:** "1807 El 2 [OCR '2%'] de febrero temblor fuerte en Jauja." Then **"1808 … Temblor en Lima que duró seis segundos, con extraordinario movimiento."** No day or month appears in the snippet. Then "1810" (OCR "1510"): "El 14 de noviembre, á las 11 y 45 p. m., temblor fuerte, que duró 30 m. [sic, read s.] en Arica"; "El 26 de diciembre … temblor fuerte en Arequipa".
- **There is no entry for 1809**: "1809" returns 0 hits in t. 8.
- **No volcano entries from p. 376 onward.** The searches for volcán and ceniza hit only pp. 356–375. The relevant one: **p. 375: "Lluvia de ceniza en la Paz, durante los días 27, 28 y 29 de agosto"**, falling on Chuquingora (Pacajes) and killing livestock, "Se atribuye á un volcán de la costa, camino de Moquegua á la Paz, cerca de la laguna Iscunchaca". It sits between the 1792 and 1797 entries, so it dates to **c. 1792–1796** and falls outside the window. It is noted as a precedent for ash falls on La Paz from a southern Peruvian source (Ubinas or Tutupaca?). Pp. 370–373 cover Misti throwing out "tierra" towards Chiguata (the 1784–87 sequence).
- t. 9 (https://archive.org/details/boletndelasocied9189soci) carries the 1861–1898 continuation. Its 1808–09 hits are a **missionary itinerary on the Ucayali** (fray Tomás Alcántara?, Pueblo Nuevo → Sarayacu → Cuntamana, 11 Jun 1808 – 8 Oct 1809, pp. 480–502). Searches turned up no haze or sky remarks, but **only dates were searched**. Follow-up: the itinerary is the "Extracto del viaje que hizo el Reverendo Padre Misionero Apostólico Fray Tomás Alcántara de la Orden de San Francisco…" (pp. 480–518). The manuscript came from J. T. Polo and is held in the library of the Sociedad Geográfica de Lima (p. 480). It is an **extract, not a daily diary**: the dated entries jump from 27 Jul 1808 (arrival at Sarayacu) to 16 Jan 1809 (departure from Sarayacu), so nothing is dated for Dec 1808. Searches for "humo" (only p. 485, tobacco smoke) and "enero" found no sky or haze remark. This is a weak lead. The original manuscript (Soc. Geogr. Lima) could be fuller.
