## F. Bottom line / next steps for a human
1. **Morier, Appendix III (IA pp. 498–501)**: read the page images (https://archive.org/details/10861100bsb, pages n497–n501) and transcribe the daily Bushire journal for 1–17 Dec 1808 and the march to Shiraz (17–30 Dec 1808). The questions are on which days "Great haze", "Haze over the mountains" and "haze all over" occur, and whether any remark on sunrise or sunset colour appears after ~5 Dec 1808. Compare with the 1807 journal (pp. 492–497), where the haze was also "such … as I have not remarked in former times", to judge how normal the haze was.
2. **Elphinstone 1809**: "the summer of 1809 was reckoned very cool" (Peshawar) is a modest climate datum. The mission's thermometer diaries (BL Elphinstone papers) could give numbers.
3. **Beruti** (Buenos Aires): read for Dec 1808 – Jun 1809 → no natural phenomena. This is now a documented negative.
4. Still open: *Minerva Peruana* Dec 1808–Feb 1809 (check the Notre Dame MARBLE Mirador viewer by hand); Montessus (anales.uchile.cl, open in a normal browser); Moreau de Jonnès *Histoire physique des Antilles* (Manioc).
