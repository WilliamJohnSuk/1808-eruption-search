## Wexford (EIC) journal, BL IOR/L/MAR/B/173C, images 0221-0246: weather transcription

Coverage: **Sun 18 Dec 1808 – Sat 25 Mar 1809**. Table Bay (Cape of Good Hope) 18 Dec 1808 – 12 Feb 1809; sailed 13 Feb; St Helena Roads 24 Feb – 28 Feb; sailed 1 Mar; passed Ascension 7 Mar; crossed the Equator about 12 Mar; in the doldrums at about 2–4 N, 18–21 W through 25 Mar. No crew or passenger list pages are in this range.

Journal days are nautical days (noon to noon, PM first). In port the journal is continuous prose with no position or thermometer, so those cells show "—". Uncertain readings are marked [?]. At-sea "Lon in" is the longitude in.

## Target-phenomena flags

No dimmed, red, pale or copper sun or moon. No dry fog, smoke, ash or dust, sulphur, pumice, earthquake or unexplained reports or detonations. No unusual sunsets or twilights. The only items that come near the search criteria are routine:

- **0224, Thu 19 Jan 1809, Table Bay:** "Light variable Winds from the N.W. & hot sultry Weather thro't the Day, in the Night thick Fog". This is ordinary Cape sea fog.
- **0225/0226, Wed 1 Feb 1809, Table Bay:** "...middle and latter thick foggy Weather".
- **0227, Mon 13 Feb 1809, leaving Table Bay:** "Light variable Winds from the N.W. and thick foggy Weather". The Winds column has "Thick Fog", "heard a Gun B[earin]g W[es]t[?]" and "Very thick Fog, being near the Rocks of Penguin Island". The gun was a convoy fog signal, not a phenomenon.
- **"Hazy" in the Winds column at 1 PM:** 0228 (14 Feb, 32°S 16°E), 0232 (23 Feb, 17°S 3°W), 0238 (9 Mar, 3°S 17°W) and 0239 (10 Mar, 2°S 17°W). In the Remarks, **0229 (16 Feb, 28°39'S 12°17'E)** reads "with hazy Weather". These are typical SE-trade haze, with nothing about colour, sun or moon.
- **0244, Tue 21 Mar 1809 (3°45'N 19°59'W):** "Hard Rain Thunder & Lightg" and "with much Lightning during the Night". This is ITCZ convection.
- Other "guns" and "blue lights" were convoy signals, for example Thais being recalled with 2 guns on 4 Jan and the Commodore burning blue lights.
