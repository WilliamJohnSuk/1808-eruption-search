**Sir Francis Drake (U71), George Harris.** Horsburgh: examined Adoomatis Atoll, Maldives, 27 Jul 1808; "His Majesty's ship Drake, September 1st, 1809, struck on a small coral shoal" off Mount Ophir on the west coast of Sumatra (about 0° N, 100° E); with Cornelia at the Cargados Carajos shoals (16°47'S, 59°34'E) in Jan 1810. So the "Java/Madura blockade" in U71 is 1810-11, and her Nov 1808-Mar 1809 position is unverified, probably the Bay of Bengal or Sumatra side [inference]. Yield lowered to L.

**Psyche (U73), John Edgcumbe.** Marshall's RNB: Persian Gulf May-Jul 1808 with Malcolm's embassy; then, under Captain Charles Foote (Piemontaise), with Colonel St Leger against the Travancore rising, silencing a battery, destroying vessels and taking two vessels carrying elephants; the rising ran from the attack on the Cochin Residency on 28 Dec 1808 to the armistice of 26 Feb 1809 (Wikipedia, Velu Thampi), and Horsburgh vol. 1 prints Edgcumbe's soundings off Cape Comorin and Manapar. So Psyche was on the Malabar and Tinnevelly coasts in Dec 1808-Feb 1809, and the master's log ADM 52/3787 (13 Apr 1808-12 Apr 1809) covers that. Then "he accompanied Captain Cole in search of two French frigates reported in the China seas" (RNB), the Manila mission: Cole was still at Penang on 3 Apr 1809 (ADM 1/1940/54), and Drury's China letters of Oct-Nov 1809 mention Doris and Psyche in the China seas (ADM 1/1453/50).
