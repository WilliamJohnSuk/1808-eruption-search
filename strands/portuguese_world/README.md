**Question.** Do Portuguese and Brazilian sources, or Spanish and Portuguese ships' papers, record anything in 1808-09? Did any Iberian ship pass a candidate volcano?

**What was read.** Portuguese and Brazilian periodicals and books (OCR searches); Spanish naval commanders' reports in the Biblioteca Virtual de Defensa; the track charts of the *Ramoncita* (Lima to Manila, 1809), page images.

**Main results**
- We found no Portuguese-language description of a strange sky, haze, dimmed or red sun between Nov 1808 and mid-1809, but the key papers (*Gazeta do Rio de Janeiro* 1809, *Gazeta de Lisboa*, *Diario Lisbonense*) could not be searched online, so this is an access gap rather than a negative.
- The only eruption in the Lusophone world in 1808-09 is São Jorge, Azores (May-June 1808), far too small.
- **F32** [page image]: the *Ramoncita*'s charts show her track through the Surigao Strait past Panaon, about 15-20 km from Cabalían (Claude's distance), between 21 Jan and 11 Feb 1809. We found no ash, pumice or haze noted on them; charts carry navigation, not weather, so this is a very weak negative.

**Open.** The *Ramoncita*'s diary for Jan-Apr 1809 is the most valuable unread source in the project (U151, T26). The Lisbon and Rio papers.
