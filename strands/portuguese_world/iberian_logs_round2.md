# Iberian ships' logs and voyage reports, Nov 1808 – Jun 1809 (round 2, strand "iberian_logs")

Compiled 16 Oct 2026. Scope: Spanish and Portuguese ships at sea or in tropical ports between Nov 1808 and Jun 1809 whose logs, diaries or voyage reports could hold sky, haze, ash or pumice remarks. This file lists **what exists and where**. **No sky, haze or ash remark was found** in anything read from here. Nothing below is a volcanic observation yet. These are leads for a human to open.

## 0. Main finding: a searchable, digitised archive

**The Biblioteca Virtual de Defensa (BVD) holds digitised AGMAB "Expediciones a Indias" papers for 1808–1809.** These are naval commanders' reports from Callao, Montevideo, Cartagena, San Blas and Manila, item-catalogued and imaged, with shelfmarks in the form `AHA AB 000LL.NNN` (Archivo Histórico de la Armada "Álvaro de Bazán", legajo.document). The BVD also holds Museo Naval (AMN/AHA-Elcano) manuscripts and charts.
- Search URL pattern that works via WebFetch: `https://bibliotecavirtual.defensa.gob.es/BVMDefensa/es/consulta/resultados_busqueda.do?busq_general=<terms>&busq_periodo=<year>`
- Viewer: `https://bibliotecavirtual.defensa.gob.es/BVMDefensa/es/catalogo_imagenes/grupo.do?path=<n>`. The viewer page loaded and listed thumbnails, but neither the full images nor the METS export could be read from here. A human can read and download them in a browser ("Descargar grupo").
- These are mostly **arrival reports and covering letters**, not full logbooks. A commander's arrival report (*parte de llegada*) usually summarises the passage, and sometimes encloses an *extracto del diario*. The 1808–09 *diarios de navegación* themselves were not found catalogued online.

## 2. Portuguese side: what exists
- **CLIWOC contains no Portuguese logs.** Können & Koek (2005): "Portugal ... was not involved", because "logbooks from the period after 1755 could not be located" (docta.ucm.es PDF, read).
- **Arquivo Histórico da Marinha (Lisbon)**, códices index (Índice 30, documentação encadernada) has *diários náuticos* only for 1800 (bergantim *Boa Ventura*, no. 1127), 1801 (*Serpente*, registo diário, 1134), 1802/03 (*Balão*, registo de quartos, 1126), 1803 (*Condessa de Rezende*, 1128), then **1810 (bergantim *Vingança*, no. 1138, loc. 6-VII-6-2)**. There is **nothing for 1808–1809**. The 1807–1808 Navy logs evidently went to Brazil with the fleet. URL: https://cultura.marinha.pt/pt/biblioteca_web/arquivohistorico_web/fundoscolecoes_web/Documents/ÍNDICE%2030%20-%20Códices%20%20-%20Documentação%20Encadernada.pdf . The "Navios Antigos" catalogue has nothing for 1807–1810. The "Documentação encadernada – Brasil" catalogue has only Conselho do Almirantado registers for 1809 (PT/BCM-AH/DOC ENC/CONSALM/002/539).
- The **Archeevo search** (https://arquivohistorico.marinha.pt/) ignores URL query parameters, so it was not searchable from here.
- Likely real repositories for 1808–09 Portuguese Navy logs: **Arquivo Nacional (Rio)**, Marinha fonds; **DPHDM / Arquivo da Marinha (Rio)**; **AHU Lisbon**, Conselho Ultramarino caixas for Macau, Índia, Moçambique, Brasil (digitarq; record URLs redirect to the portal home from here); **Biblioteca Nacional (Rio)**, manuscripts.
- Relevant 1808–09 Portuguese movements: the Cayenne expedition from Pará (Dec 1808 – Jan 1809, with British HMS *Confiance*); Rio–Goa/Macau sailings; the 1809 *Ulysses* (above).
