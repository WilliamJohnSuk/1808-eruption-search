## 1. Bottom line

The Grokipedia page "1808 mystery eruption" mixes correct facts from Guevara-Murua et al. 2014 and the Global Volcanism Program with claims that are wrong, untraced, or pinned to the wrong footnote. Of the 51 claims we listed, we checked 26 that bear on the source, date or nature of the eruption. Of those 26, 9 are supported by the source cited, 14 are misattributed, mislabelled, overstated or contradicted, and 3 are untraced, unchecked or contradict the page itself. Both of its named "proposed sources" are weak. The Agrigan "October 2025 proposal" has no publication behind it that we could find. The Tambo Quemado "2023 Oregon State zircon project" is a real student project about a differently named volcano ("Cerro Volcán Quemado"), and the project page never mentions 1808. Grokipedia's own Agrigan page does not mention 1808 at all.

One useful thing came out of the check (F39). The live GVP page now lists a confirmed **1808 eruption of Karthala** (Comoros). It is not in the May 2018 GVP snapshot the project has used so far, which means our GVP screen is out of date (T59).
