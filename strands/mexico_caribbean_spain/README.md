**Question.** Is there a sky or eruption report around the Caribbean, in New Spain, Central America or Spain, and could a Central American volcano be the source?

**What was read.** The *Gazeta de México*, *Diario de México*, *Gazeta de Madrid*, *Diario de Mallorca* and *Gaceta de Caracas* page by page for the window (the `*_reading_passes` folders hold one file per batch of issues); Caribbean and Guianas papers; Montessus, Arreola and other Central American catalogues; the San Fernando (Cádiz) observatory register.

**Main results**
- We found no report of a dim, pale or silver sun, haze, unusual twilight, ash, pumice or detonations in these papers for Dec 1808-Jun 1809 (page images for the Madrid, Mallorca and Mexico papers; OCR for the Caribbean ones).
- **F14** [computed]: hazy ("calimoso") days at San Fernando in Jan-Mar 1809 all came with east to south-east winds, so probably Saharan dust (a negative).
- Central America as the source is physically possible but unsupported: an eruption of this size would be the region's largest between about 1360 and 1902 and has left no deposit or record.
- Small eruptions ruled out as the source: Taal (Feb-Apr 1808), Colima (effusive), "Cosigüina 1809" (rests on 1836 hearsay).

**Open.** The *Gazeta de Guatemala* for 1808-09 (no digitized copy found).
