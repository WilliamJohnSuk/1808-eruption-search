Do not count them: "misa de rogativa" (Cabildo of Valladolid de Michoacán, 8 Aug, 1808-10-01_p09.jpg, p.735) and "rogativas públicas" (Real de Pinos, 28 Aug, Num. 107, pp.747-750). The packets sent toward Manila had been recalled. This is political, not shipping news, and there is no other news from Manila or Acapulco this month.
