## Hits
Nothing in the range mentions a dim or odd sun or moon, red skies, haze or dry fog, ash, pumice, volcanoes, earthquakes, meteors or aurora. The only weather items are two reports of deep snow in the Sierra de Guadarrama during the Napoleon / Moore campaign of late December 1808:

1. **22 Jan 1809, p. 92 (18090122-4.jpg)**. "Carta de un sugeto de la mayor recomendacion, dirigida desde Iviza á esta Isla con fecha de 19 de Enero de 1809" (report brought by the Alicante mail).
   > "La Batalla grande fue en el campo de Pellegeros, en donde los franceses no pudieron pasar su artilleria por las muchas nieves del Guadarrama."
   Gloss: the big battle was at the "campo de Pellegeros", where the French could not get their artillery through because of the heavy snow on the Guadarrama.

2. **23 Jan 1809, p. 94 (18090123-2.jpg)**. "Noticias publicada por el Gobierno de Alicante", account of Don Galo Garcia, who left Madrid on 29 Dec (dated Alicante 8 de Enero de 1809, p. 95).
   > "Que el plan, y accion por los Franceses fue mandada por el Emperador … Que los Franceses por las nieves no pudieron pasar la Artilleria por el Puerto: Que el ataque fué el 24 y el 25 …"
   Gloss: because of the snow the French could not get their artillery over the pass (the Guadarrama pass); the attack was on 24–25 December.

(The 28 Jan issue, p. 115, Madrid 24 de Diciembre, says only that "salieron como 30 mil Franceses para Castilla por Guadarrama", with no weather. The 29 Jan footnote on p. 118 says "cerca de Guadarrama", also with no weather. Neither counts as a hit.)

## Other notes
- 17 Jan issue (observed 16 Jan): a sudden cold snap. The thermometer fell from about 12½ to 7½–8½ °R and the wind turned NNE/NNO/NO, with the barometer rising from 27p11 to 28p1. These are the lowest temperatures in the range.
- 31 Jan issue, page 1: the sunset is misprinted as "se pone las 5 y . m. 12. s." (minutes left blank, and 5 where 4 is expected). Typesetting error only.
- 21 Jan issue, p. 88: an English laúd reports that a British ship of the line at Mahón captured 14 of 20 sail in a convoy from Marseille to Barcelona. No weather.
- 26 Jan issue, p. 107–108: "En Barcelona reyna la mayor miseria" (Barcelona is in the greatest misery) because of a shortage of grain and meat. The cause given is the war (siege), not the weather.
- 23 Jan issue, p. 96: smallpox vaccine has been brought back to Mallorca from Valencia by D. Francisco Gil. Not weather.
- 25 and 29 Jan issues: market prices for wheat, barley and oats ("Precios del trigo"). Olive oil prices appear daily at about 18 sueldos. These could be used as a proxy series.
