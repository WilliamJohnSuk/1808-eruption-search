## Other notes
- Some tables look like they were not reset between issues, so treat them with caution. The temperature and pressure figures are the same in the 16, 17, 18 and 19 Dec issues. The 19 Dec table is identical to the 18 Dec one, sunrise and sunset times included ("7 y 23 m. 51 s." / "4 y 36 m. 5 s."), so it is almost certainly a reprint. The 24 and 25 Dec issues also share identical temperature and pressure figures and differ only in wind.
- 22 Dec issue (obs. 21 Dec), "NEE." is printed as such, probably meaning ENE.
- 28 Dec issue (obs. 27 Dec): the leading "2" is dropped in two of the three pressure readings, which are printed "7 p. 8 l." and "7 p. 8½[?] l.". I read them as 27p8l.
- Cold spell in the obs. dates 20-25 Dec: temperatures were 5-6.5 °R all day with almost no daily range, against 8.5-10 °R in mid-December and 9-10.5 °R from the 26th to the 30th.
- Pressure dropped sharply from 28p10l (obs. 20 Dec) to 27p9l (obs. 21 Dec), then fell further to 27p5l by the afternoon of 23 Dec. This points to a stormy, unsettled spell around the solstice, with N/NE winds turning W/SW.
- Sunrise times are a little irregular: 7h24m from the 20 to 23 Dec issues, then 7h23m59s on 24 Dec and decreasing after that (7h22m38s on 31 Dec). This is probably just how the almanac was computed and is not a sky observation.
- 20 Dec issue, p.4 (18081220-4.jpg), Perdida: "El Edictor de este Periódico perdió no sabe donde el último sabado su paraguas" (the editor lost his umbrella last Saturday, 17 Dec). This hints at rain or need of an umbrella, which is weak evidence at best.
- 27 Dec issue, p.2: an item headed "Granada 30 de Diciembre" (sic; presumably 30 Nov) is about requisitioning horses and has no weather content.
- Ships arrived at Palma regularly throughout the period (from Alicante, Cartagena, Mataró, Mahón, Málaga, Ibiza, Almería, Valencia, Gibraltar, Tarragona). None of the notices mentions a storm, delay or damage.
