(probably 28p3l, given 28p3l at noon). 21 Sep issue, 7h thermometer "28 g." (probably 18) and barometer "27 p. 5 l." The 16 and 17 Sep issues print identical thermometer and barometer readings.
- Pressure fell on 23 Sep (28p1l to 27p11l, winds shifting to ESE and SSE), stayed low at 27p9l on 24 Sep with S and SSO winds, and fell again to 27p10-11l on 28-29 Sep. Temperatures dropped from about 20° R (19-20 Sep) to 15-16° R (28-29 Sep). That is an ordinary autumn change.
- 23 Sep, p.3 (18080923-3.jpg), "Denia 18 de Septiembre": "el Patron del correo que estuvo detenido en este puerto por el viento contrario" — the mail boat was held in port by a contrary wind.
- 23 Sep, p.2 (18080923-2.jpg), letter from Tuy dated 21 June: "El calor del Sol, la humedad del rocío" — routine discomforts of the journey.
- 25 Sep, p.4 (18080925-4.jpg), Zaragoza letter of 17 Aug: "corria la sangre por las calles como quando llueve mucho" — a simile, not weather.
