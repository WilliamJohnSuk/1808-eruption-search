- **Disease, Pamplona (Nov 25, p.4):** the "Zaragoza 15 de Noviembre" report says the French army left Pamplona to camp outside because of the many sick, with 70–80 deaths a day. This is disease, not weather.
- **Disease, Pamplona (Nov 26, p.3):** the "Pamplona 11 de Noviembre" report says: "En esta Ciudad hay una epidemia, que hace muchos estragos en el exército frances; y se asegura, que sus enfermos ascienden á 7 mil."
- **Cathedral procession (Nov 19, p.4):** an Aviso announces a procession and an eight-day Mission "para aplacar la ira de Dios en las calamidades que nos oprimen". The context is the war, and no weather is mentioned.
- **Prose ode "La Philantropia" (Nov 23, pp.2–3):** it is allegorical and contains sky imagery ("aquella brillante nube que desciende del firmamento", "Ya el sol apagó sus rayos"). It is literary, not an observation.
- **Rhetorical mentions:** Villafranca 24 Oct (Nov 28, p.3) uses a sun metaphor. Ney's proclamation, from Coruña 12 Nov (Nov 30, pp.3–4), praises Spain's "dulce, suave y variado clima", and a footnote calls the Pyrenees "un pais desierto y frio". All of these are rhetorical.
- **Azores:** the Behaim/Otto memoir (Nov 18–22) mentions Fayal and the Azores only in a historical context. Nothing in it is volcanic.
