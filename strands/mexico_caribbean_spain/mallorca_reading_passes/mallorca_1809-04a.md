## Other notes
- **Cold spell 5–9 April**: readings fell from 11.5–13.5 R on 4 April to 6–7 R on 7–9 April, with N to NE winds and high pressure (28p3–4l). Temperatures were back to 10–13 R by the 13th–16th.

- **14 April, p. 2**: a letter datelined "Cagliari en Cerdeña 16 de Febrero" begins "Hemos arribado por el tiempo", i.e. they put in at Cagliari because of the weather.
- **18 April, p. 2**: news from Madrid (via the Gazeta de Valencia) reports deaths "de las calenturas estacionales que reynan" (the seasonal fevers now going round), and many French sick in the Madrid hospitals.
- **Hunger stories caused by war, not weather**:
  - 1 April, p. 4, Granada 2 de Marzo: the French in Ferrol and Coruña, "su caballeria perece por falta de alimento, y la gente va á sufrir una hambre espantosa" (their cavalry is dying for lack of food and the people face a terrible famine).
  - 14 April, p. 4, Granada 27 de Marzo: the French are dying of an epidemic "producida de la escasez de pan, aceyte, y otros efectos" (caused by shortage of bread, oil and other goods); rye bread costs 20 reales a pound.
- **Zaragoza epidemic (siege)**, 4–5 April: "una epidemia mortal y aguda", over 16,000 sick.
