   Gloss: on 22 May 1809 a sudden Danube flood near Vienna broke Napoleon's bridges. The French bulletin blamed snowmelt brought forward by "early heat of the last few days", about a month ahead of the usual mid-June flood.

"Tempestate" here is figurative (the storm of war), not weather.
