**Question.** Which volcanoes could have produced the 1809 eruption, and what tests can tell them apart?

**What was read.** The Global Volcanism Program list; published glass and rock analyses (GEOROC, EarthChem) scored against the 1809 ice glass [computed]; candidate profiles; sediment, coral and cave archives near the candidates; parish burial books of Bohol, as the nearest surviving test of Cabalían (page images).

**Main results**
- **Priority list** (26 Oct, unchanged): top Dakataua/Makalia (New Britain), Gaua (Vanuatu), Cabalían (Leyte); possible Agung, Todoko-Ranu, Dukono, Teon, Nila, northern Sulawesi. Ruled out: Etna, Vesuvius, Hekla, Long Island, non-volcanic causes.
- **F20, F34**: the 1809 glass is a common medium-K island-arc andesite. Many unrelated volcanoes match it equally well, including much of Vanuatu, so major elements cannot name the source. A trace-element lookup has been written down in advance for the day the ice shards are analysed.
- **F35**: we found no tephra dated to c. 1809 in 58 archives within 300 km of the candidates; only Kau Bay (Halmahera) has cores that could resolve it.
- **F27**: no excess burials after Nov 1808 in three Bohol parishes, 150-180 km from Cabalían; a weak negative for a very large eruption near Bohol. No Leyte register survives on film for before 1861.
- Long Island (PNG) is excluded as an 1808 source.

**Open.** Agung in the trace-element lookup (U154); published trace elements for southern Leyte (T28); researcher-held ice glass (U90-U92).
