**What this is.** A synthesis of results already in the register, with no new sources. **The ranking is Claude's judgment (inference)**, not a finding. Every glass score is Claude's calculation (7-oxide similarity, 22 Oct method). Scores against whole rock are flagged "WR"; whole rock always flatters a candidate, because the glass of a 60% SiO2 rock is more silicic than the rock. Every "test" line is a **suggestion**.

The holders of unpublished 1809 glass are Hutchison (St Andrews), Plunkett (Belfast) and McConnell (DRI); whether to contact them is William's decision.

## 2. Top tier

Ranked by Claude's judgment. None has evidence of an eruption in 1808. They rank highest because nothing excludes them and each has one specific thing in its favour.

### 1. Dakataua (Makalia cone), New Britain, PNG; 5.1°S; GVP tier B
- **Glass:** Makalia groundmass glass (Lowder 1970, sample 114G) **0.92 Dome C**, 0.89 South Pole. This is the only glass-to-glass score at the top level from a blank-zone volcano. One 1970 analysis of an undated Holocene lava, not tephra (file; B50 sec. 8).
- **Size:** caldera volcano; the Dk caldera eruption (c. 650 CE) was VEI 5-6. GVP says "at least five subplinian or vulcanian eruptions" followed, undated. Capable in principle; the post-caldera eruptions are described as smaller (B52).
- **Dated deposit near 1808:** none. The last eruption (Makalia lava and cinder cones) is dated "about 1880-1890" from 1963 village testimony.
- **Records:** we know of no written account from the Willaumez Peninsula in 1808 that has reached the archives. People lived there and kept their history orally (the Makalia date itself comes from 1963 village testimony); B48/B51 silence carries almost no weight here.
- **Ice:** south of the equator (small plus).
- **Single test (suggestion):** a short sediment core from Lake Dakataua, dated by 210Pb/14C, to see whether an ash layer of c. 1800-1810 exists next to the vents. Desk step first: Machida et al. 1996 (paywalled, in download_links.md), which should list the post-caldera eruptions and any dates.

### 2. Gaua (Banks Islands, Vanuatu); 14.3°S; GVP tier B
- **Glass:** no glass. WR 0.94 South Pole, 0.91 Dome C (sample SM30A, Santa Maria pyroclastic series); 68 analyses, K2O median 2.35 (file; B50 sec. 9, B52).
- **Size:** 6 × 9 km caldera (Lake Letas), formed by the Santa Maria pyroclastic eruptions; undated.
- **Dated deposit near 1808:** none. No dated eruption before 1962 apart from solfataric activity from 1868.
- **Records:** no European visitor left a written account between Quirós (1606) and the later 19th-century missions, though the islands were inhabited throughout; Vanuatu oral history is part of William's eHRAF search.
- **Ice:** furthest south of the top candidates (small plus).
- **Single test (suggestion):** date the youngest tephra on the caldera rim or in Lake Letas sediment. Desk step: Robin, Eissen & Monzier 1995 (Terra Nova 7:436) for the age of the caldera-forming series.

### 3. Cabalían (southern Leyte, Philippines); 10.3°N; GVP tier A
- **Glass:** **no composition at all** (not in GEOROC or Gill files). GVP calls it andesite.
- **Size:** unknown. "Explosion and pyroclastic flow", no VEI given; suggests small to moderate (inference). This is its weakness.
- **Dated deposit near 1808:** **the only candidate with one.** A pyroclastic-flow deposit dated 14C 135 ± 30 BP (GVP "1820 ± 30"). Calibrated, it spans roughly 1680-1940 cal AD (Claude's approximate reading), which includes 1808 but also most of two centuries.
- **Records:** a Spanish mission town on Sogod Bay; parish and mission records should exist but have not been read. The printed Philippine chronicles (B48) are silent, weak negative.
- **Ice:** north of the equator (small minus).
- **Single test (suggestion):** the archival one, which fits this project's method: Cabalían and Sogod parish books and Leyte mission correspondence for 1808-09 (Franciscan/Augustinian archives, or someone in the Philippines). Science alternative: find and re-date the 135 BP sample (COMVOL 1981, PHIVOLCS 2007 hazard map) and get its glass.
