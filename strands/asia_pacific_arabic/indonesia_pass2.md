## 1. Agung / "Carang Assam", c. 1808

### 1.1 Earliest printing of the Raffles Tambora narrative, with the c. 1808 Bali passage

**Original printing found: *Verhandelingen van het Bataviaasch Genootschap van Kunsten en Wetenschappen*, vol. 8 (Batavia 1816).** The volume also carries the English title "Transactions of the Batavian Society of Arts and Sciences, Vol. VIII". Its table of contents lists the paper on the eruption of the "Tomboro" mountain on Sumbawa (OCR "Somboro"), near the end of the volume.

- **IA `verhandelingend04wetegoog`** (Oxford copy, metadata date 1816). fts snippets:
  - "when an eruption took place from the Volcano of Carang Assam in Bali about 7 years a?o, and it was at"
  - "extont.—• This person states that the Volcano of Carang Assam in Rali, wad in commotion at th* samé 4ime"
- **IA `verhandelingen12wetegoog`** (Michigan copy; metadata "volume 8", date 1826, probably a reissue). In-book search puts the passages at **p. 360 and p. 361** of 371 pages. fts snippets:
  - "when an erupcion took place from the Volcano of Carang Assam in Bali abouc 7 years ago, and ir was ac"
  - "in extent —Tiiis person states that cbe Volcano of Carang Assam in Bali, was in comraocion ac the same"
  - p. 360, paraphrased by the summariser (it would not quote at length): all accounts agree that no eruption this violent had occurred within memory or tradition. A lesser eruption of "the Volcano of Carang Assam in Bali" about 7 years earlier was at first supposed to be the source. The **next sentence** says Balinese linked the 1815 disaster to a recent quarrel between the **two Rajahs of Buleleng**, which ended in the younger Raja's death on his brother's orders.
  - p. 361, paraphrased: the captain of a Malay prow reported total darkness near Sumbawa, ash several feet deep, and floating cinders. He stated that "the Volcano of Carang Assam in Bali" was **"in commotion at the same time"**, i.e. in April 1815. No matching activity was found along Java's volcanic chain.

### 1.2 Secondary chain (embellishment)
- **Junghuhn 1857**, Bd. 2, p. 909 (pass 1): "1808. In diesem Jahre warf er eine ungeheure Menge Asche nebst Bimstein aus". He cites Raffles only. **The exact year (1815 − 7) and "immense ash and pumice" are Junghuhn's own additions.**
- **Buddingh**, *Neêrlands-Oost-Indië. Reizen…* (1859), p. 446 (IA `nerlandsoostind00buddgoog`), on the Agong as the "hoogsten berg des eilands": "welks uitbarsting van 1808 zoo veel rampen stichtte" (OCR "zoo leel ramps"). In English: "whose eruption of 1808 caused so many disasters". No source is given.
- ***Aardrijkskundig en statistisch woordenboek van Ned. Indië*** vol. 1 (1861), p. 16. The entry "Agong, Abang, Goenong Karang Asam of Piek van Bali, vuurspuwende berg…" has (fts) "berg is vooral door eene uitbarsting in ]S08 bekend geworden", i.e. the mountain is known above all for an eruption in 1808.
- **Neumann van Padang 1951 → Simkin et al. → GVP** "Agung 1808, VEI 2".
  - Simkin's footnote "(eruption in 1808, not 1810 or 1811)" and NvP's sentence "The eruptions mentioned of 1810 and 1811 seem to relate to 1808" both belong to **Ruang**, not Agung. In Simkin's listing the footnote sits next to "Ruang (Sangihe Is) … 1808 1836? 1840".

### 1.3 Independent Dutch description of Bali (1848)
***Het eiland Balie en de Balienezen*** (Amsterdam 1848), IA `heteilandbailie00lautgoog`:
- p. 27: "Lange was de Agoeng in rust geweest, toen , in het jaar 1843, door trillingen en schuddingen eene ophanden zijnde uitbarsting werd aangekondigd." In English: Agung had long been quiet before 1843. **No 1808 eruption is mentioned.**
- p. 26: "In het jaar 1804 heeft de Djembrana de omliggende landstreek met gloeijende asch en steenen overdekt. Over een' omtrek van eenige uren was de schade aanzienlijk". Spoiled water and foul air followed, and epidemics "raapten een niet gering gedeelte der bevolking weg".
- p. 137: "deze plaats in 1804 door de uitbarsting van den vulkaan Sjembrana veel geleden heeft … toen in het jaar 1808 de uitroeijing der Boegienezen oorzaak was, dat ze voor een gedeelte vernield werd". In English: in 1808 the extermination of the Bugis, a war, partly destroyed Jembrana.
- p. 26: the Batur eruption of **1818** caused heavy damage.
- **Note:** western Bali (Jembrana) has no historically active volcano. The "1804 Djembrana eruption" is unexplained: it may be a misplaced or misdated event. It is 11 years before 1815, not 7.

### 1.4 Assessment: what "Agung 1808" rests on
- **Primary basis: one hearsay sentence.** Raffles's officers recorded it in April–May 1815. Unspecified residents "speak of similar effects in a lesser degree" from the Karangasem volcano "about 7 years ago". That places the event at **c. 1806–1810**.
- **Scale:** the effects were noticed at a distance (detonations and/or ash). That points to a moderate explosive eruption. No VEI is supportable.
- **The year 1808, the "immense ash and pumice" (Junghuhn) and the "many disasters" (Buddingh) are later accretions without new sources.** The VEI 2 is a catalogue default.
- **Counter-evidence:** the 1848 Bali description implies Agung had been quiet for a long time before 1843.
- **Informant habit:** the same 1816 narrative records an informant who put Agung "in commotion" during Tambora in April 1815. Informants readily attributed detonations to Agung.

## 2. Lamongan, "8 Dec 1808": the source chain

### 2.1 Neumann van Padang 1951, the immediate source for GVP
- *Catalogue of the Active Volcanoes of the World including Solfatara Fields, Part I* (1951). IA **`bwb_KU-093-918`**: restricted lending copy. fts snippets work; in-book search failed.
- The Lamongan table OCR is garbled across columns: "1849 O f @ => June - Sept. 1806 o + May 1856 © f March | - June 14 1808 o j; Dec. 8 1859 O { ce Fekr".
- Simkin et al. code the same entries as "1806 05 LAMONGAN (JAVA)" and "1808 1208 LAMONGAN (JAVA)". That reading gives **1806 May** and **1808 Dec 8**.
  - Copies: `volcanoesofworld0000unse` (1981), `…simk` (1994), `…sieb` (2010).
- NvP's cited source for these entries was **not readable**.

### 2.2 Older chronology with a one-year offset: Kluge 1863
- E. Kluge, *Ueber Synchronismus und Antagonismus von vulkanischen Eruptionen…* (1863), IA `10134034bsb`, chronological table at **p. 72**. In this OCR "4" is often printed for "1".
  - "1805. Lamongan im Mai. . | 1806. Ba. Ders, a6 41807. Berapi auf Sumatra . | 1807. Lamongan am 8. De-[cember]"
  - The same page has "4848. Lamongan am 8. No-[vember]", i.e. 1848. Elsewhere: "1797. Lamongan" and "Lamongan 1799" (p. 21).
- **Kluge gives May 1805 and 8 Dec 1807. NvP 1951 gives May 1806 and 8 Dec 1808.** The day and month are identical in both entries and the years are shifted by exactly one, so this is the same pair of records.
  - Either one of the compilations shifted both years, or the two-column OCR misaligned Kluge's year labels.
  - **The page image needs checking.**
- **Junghuhn 1857**, Bd. 2, p. 670: "der benachbarte G.-Lamongan, der wie es scheint, seit 1806 auch nicht geruht hatte". An in-book search of Bd. 2 for "Lamongan" and "December" found no 8 December date in 1799–1812. The Dutch edition (1850–54) was not searched.
- ***Het vulkanologisch onderzoek in Indonesië*** (1950): Lamongan (no. 49) eruption years "1799, 1806, 1808, 1818, 1821, …". The index by year reads: "1806 49, 51 … 1807 9, 28, 41? … 1808 49, 54".

### 2.3 Assessment
- "8 Dec 1808" is **not a misreading of 1838 or 1898**. It is a specific record that already existed by 1863, when Kluge printed it as **8 Dec 1807** (if the OCR column reading holds). **The year is therefore 1807 or 1808.**
- The record probably comes from a Dutch Residency report (Besuki or Probolinggo), passed on through Junghuhn's Dutch edition, Hoffmann, or the *Natuurkundig Tijdschrift* lists. **The original has not been located.**
- A Lamongan explosion is necessarily small (cinder cone and maars), so it is irrelevant to the bipolar sulfate signal. If the true year is 1807, it also falls outside the Guevara-Murua window.
- **Possible conflation:** Lamongan is about 100 km across the Bali Strait from Agung. Detonations that East Java residents remembered in 1815 as "Carang Assam, about 7 years ago" might have included Lamongan activity in 1806–1808. This is unproven.
