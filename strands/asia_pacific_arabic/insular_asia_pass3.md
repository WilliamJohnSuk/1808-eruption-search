- Source: F. Junghuhn, *Java, deszelfs gedaante, bekleeding en inwendige structuur*, Dutch edition, vol. 2 (Amsterdam 1850). IA `mobot31753000592649`: https://archive.org/details/mobot31753000592649
- Section "15. GOENTOER. (Donderberg)" begins p. 416 ("Behalve de Lemongan is de Goenoeng Goentoer de werkzaamste der vulkanen op Java").
- **p. 420** (OCR): "1807, 1–6 September, (Tr.) — 1809, 9 Mei, (Tr.) — 1815, 15 Augustus, (Tr.) — 1816, 21 September, (Tr.)"
- **p. 420, footnote 2** (OCR, fragment): "De berigten omtrent de uitbarstingen door (Tr.) aangewezen, zijn slechts gegrond op mondelinge mededeelingen" — i.e. the eruptions marked (Tr.) rest only on oral reports (the summariser read the continuation as from chiefs and priests at "Trogon" = Tarogong, the district at Guntur's foot).
- Interpretation: this is the source of GVP "Guntur 1807 Sep" and "Guntur 1809 May 9". **Event date 9 May 1809 (oral tradition, recorded c. 1837–1848; publication 1850).** Not a December date. Guntur eruptions are small (VEI 1–2), NH-tropical (7.1°S actually SH-tropical, but small). Cannot be the 1808/09 sulfate source; at most a local ash event. Verification: OCR via in-book search; page image not seen.

### 1.2 Raung / Ijen (East Java, opposite Bali): ash eruptions under Banyuwangi commandant J. C. von Wikkerman (1800–1808) — Sapper 1917
- Source: Karl Sapper, *Katalog der geschichtlichen Vulkanausbrüche* (Strassburg 1917). IA `KatalogDerGeschichtlichenVulkanausbrueche`: https://archive.org/details/KatalogDerGeschichtlichenVulkanausbrueche
- **p. 205–206** (Raung, "Raoen (Raoeng) … im Idjen-Gebirge am Ostende Javas"), OCR:
  - "Weitere Aschenausbrüche erfolgten in der Regierungszeit der Kommandanten C. de Harris (1787—1799)4),"
  - "J. C. von Wikkerman (1800—1808)5) und Resident A. Macleod (1815—16)."
  - Then the great eruption of Jan–Feb 1817 (after Junghuhn, Java II, S. 707ff.): detonations "wie von fernen Kanonenschüssen" from 16 Jan 1817, ash rain, lahars, "die vorher verdunkelte Luft war nur noch mit feinem Aschennebel erfüllt".
  - Source cited on p. 206: "Uitbarstingen der vulkanen Idjen en Raun", *Tijdschrift voor Indische Taal-, Land- en Volkenkunde* (1858), p. 265 ff. — this article evidently compiled the **Banyuwangi commandants' reports** and is the route to the archival originals.
- Wikkerman: commandant (later "Resident") of Banyuwangi 1800–1808 (BKI 1926 deel 82 snippet: "door den commandant te Banjoewangi J. G. van Wikkerman, die als vaandrig in 1800 te Banjoewangi"; IA `in.ernet.dli.2015.104964`).
- **Significance:** Raung/Ijen lies ~30–40 km from the Bali Strait and ~100 km from Agung. Ash eruptions "in Wikkerman's time (1800–1808)" are a **plausible real event behind the 1815 hearsay "eruption of Carang Assam in Bali about seven years ago"** (see indonesia_pass2 §1). Date is only "1800–1808"; no month. Small (VEI ≤2). Not a sulfate candidate. **Lead: read TITLV 1858 p. 265ff. (vol. 7) for the year/month of the Wikkerman-era ash fall.**
- Sapper 1917 also: **p. 209** "1808*) Auswurf ungeheurer Mengen von Asche und Bimsstein." with note "Junghuhn, Java II, S. 817. Scrope-Klöden schreibt versehentlich (S. 427) 1803 für 1808." — this is Agung, repeating Junghuhn's embellishment (see pass 2). **p. 166**: Taal "1808 März, starker Aschenausbruch (Aschendecke in der Nähe 84 cm dick)". **p. 52–53**: Etna 27 Mar–9 Apr 1809. **p. 262**: "1809/12 Zunahme der Fumarolentätigkeit" (a Central American volcano; context not read). **p. 73**: São Jorge May–June 1808.
