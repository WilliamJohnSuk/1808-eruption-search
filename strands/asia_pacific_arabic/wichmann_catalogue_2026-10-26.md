## What it means for the search

1. **No 1809 entry and nothing dated between the undated 1808 Agung entry and 5 Dec 1810.** Wichmann searched Dutch, British and German sources more thoroughly than anyone before him, including the Moluccas (Rumphius/Valentijn tradition, Perrey's Moluccan monograph, Ternate records). He found no quake, eruption, booming or sea wave in the archipelago for 1809.
2. **It is a weak negative.** Wichmann himself says (p. 7, 16) that Junghuhn found only 57 quakes for all of 1806-1844, and that the European annual earthquake lists are missing for 1809-1817. The British interregnum and the war years are a known hole. The absence fits the B48 picture: the Moluccan record is thin in exactly these years, not a confirmed blank.
3. **Agung 1808 is undated here too.** Wichmann gives no month, so it cannot be placed in Nov-Dec 1808 from this source. The footnote (no. 5, p. 76) holding his source is missing from the translation; the German scan is needed to see whether he had anything better than Raffles/Junghuhn.
4. **Nothing on sulfate or glass.** The file has no ice-core, tephra or chemistry data, so notes/glass_comparison_2026-10-22.md and data/glass_ice_1809.csv are unchanged.
5. **Small side note:** Ternate Pik (Gamalama) eruption Feb 1811 with shocks. Too late for the 1809 sulfate, but worth knowing if anyone proposes Gamalama.
