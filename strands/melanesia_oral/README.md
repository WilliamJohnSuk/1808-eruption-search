**Question.** Do oral traditions in Melanesia, eastern Indonesia or Mindanao remember a darkness or ashfall around 1808?

**What was read.** Ethnographies, travel accounts and mission literature (OCR searches); Blong's *The Time of Darkness* (1982) on the highland New Guinea ash-fall legends, read in summary.

**Main results**
- We found no darkness, ashfall or sea-wave tradition that could refer to c. 1808 in the books searched.
- Savo (Solomons) was remembered as active roughly in the 1820s-1840s; too small and probably too late.
- The New Guinea "time of darkness" legends belong to the Long Island eruption of the 1660s (Blong), not 1808.
- Ruang (Sangihe) had a small eruption dated 1808 in the Dutch literature and 1811 locally.

**Note on copyright.** Blong 1982 is a modern copyrighted book; the notes paraphrase it and quote only short passages (task T35).
