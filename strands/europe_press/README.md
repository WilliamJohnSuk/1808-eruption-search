**Question.** Did European or US newspapers, scientific journals or travellers report the veil, or news of an eruption, between late 1808 and mid-1810?

**What was read.** Dutch, German, British, French, Italian, Swedish and US papers and journals (OCR full-text searches, with some page images); Luke Howard's London weather notes; retrospective dry-fog lists; eyewitness travel books published 1809-1830.

**Main results**
- We found no report of a dry fog, whitish sky, pale or red sun or abnormal twilight for Dec 1808-Jun 1809 in the European journals and papers read, nor any news of a distant eruption. Most of the reading was of OCR text, so the negative is moderate.
- **F16** [OCR]: Howard's rose-coloured haze over London on about 31 Mar-1 Apr 1809, during a cold northerly spell and a few days after Etna's eruption of 27 Mar 1809. Weak.
- Etna (Mar 1809) and Vesuvius are dated Northern Hemisphere eruptions in the window but far too small and local.
- Eyewitness books add background activity only (Tinakula 9 Jun 1809, Yasur Jul 1809, Cotopaxi continuous).
