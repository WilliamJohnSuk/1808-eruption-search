  - p. 425: "каждую ночь извергала пламя" ("threw out flame every night").
  - p. 454: "извергающее пламя и дымъ" (crater "throwing out flame and smoke").
  - p. 455: "Пламя мы видѣли всякую ночь, а днемъ только одинъ дымъ" ("We saw flame every night, but by day only smoke").
  - p. 466 (vocabulary): "Азоръ — огнедышащая гора и огонь".
  - **Interpretation:** this is normal persistent Strombolian activity at Yasur, as Cook and Forster saw in 1774. It is background activity and not a candidate event. It is independent and dated to about 26 July to 1 Aug 1809.
- **Assessment:** this is the most promising unexploited eyewitness book for the Southern Hemisphere in the core window (the Cape, Dec 1808 to Feb 1809). The OCR is currently too poor to test it.
