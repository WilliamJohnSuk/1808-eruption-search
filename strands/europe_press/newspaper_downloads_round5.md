## Hits

Nothing eruption-related so far. Weather items found (none is sky-optical):

1. *Diario de Mallorca* 8 Jan 1809, p. 35 (file DiarioDeMallorca_18090108, PDF p. 3), "Caspe 24 de Diciembre", siege of Zaragoza: "El ataque mas ó menos empeñado duró 91 horas, sin mas intermision que dos solas en la noche del miercoles, que nevó casi todo entera" (the attack paused only on the Wednesday night [21 Dec 1808], when it snowed almost all night). **Image-verified.**
2. *Diario de Mallorca* 22 Jan 1809, p. 92 (PDF p. 4), letter from Ibiza dated 19 Jan 1809: "los franceses no pudieron pasar su artilleria por las muchas nieves del Guadarrama". Read from image by a reading pass; not yet re-checked by me.
3. *Diario de Mallorca* 23 Jan 1809, p. 94 (PDF p. 2), Alicante 8 Jan 1809, account of Galo García (left Madrid 29 Dec): "los Franceses por las nieves no pudieron pasar la Artilleria por el Puerto". Read from image; not re-checked.

4. *Diario de Mallorca* 6 Feb 1809, PDF p. 3, "Granada 17 de Enero" (from the Gaceta de Valencia, 27 Jan): "en este Puerto á causa de la mucha nieve habian perecido cerca de 10 mil hombres … el Emperador abrió el paso con espada en mano, arrollando la nieve" (Guadarrama; exaggerated rumour). Read from image by a reading pass.
5. *Diario de Mallorca* 7 Feb 1809, PDF p. 3, letter from Tarragona, 31 Jan 1809: boats left Barcelona "al favor de una densisima niebla", then "sobrevino á la niebla un fuerte temporal". Read from image by a reading pass.
6. *Diario de Mallorca* 28 Feb 1809, PDF p. 3, despatch of General Cuesta, 25 Jan 1809, Puerto de Miravete (Extremadura): the advance stopped "á causa de la densa niebla que le impedia proseguir sus operaciones". Read from image by a reading pass.

7. 22 Mar 1809, PDF p. 3, "Noticias de Cataluña del 16 de Marzo": "con los temporales de levante y aguas del Cielo las Fragatas Inglesas no han podido aproxímarse para el bombeo".
8. 20 May 1809, PDF p. 1, Mequinenza, 18 Apr: casualties "por causa de una densa niebla".
9. 7 Jun 1809, PDF p. 1, intercepted French letter, Villafranca [Extremadura] 24 Apr: "los calores son aquí mas terrible que el mismo enemigo".
10. 29 Jun 1809, PDF p. 4, French bulletin on Essling (21–22 May): Danube flood "que no se verifica sino á mediados de Junio, quando se derriten las nieves, se ha adelantado por el calor anticipado". Also 25 Jun (Cinca flood, mid-May) and 19 Jun (bad weather in Portugal, mid-May).
11. 24 Nov 1808, PDF p. 4, "Cataluña 20 de Noviembre": an action abandoned "á causa de una tempestad".
12. 8 Apr 1809, PDF p. 4, "Cordoba 16 de Marzo" (Galicia): French lost two thirds of their cavalry "De resultas de las continuas y largas marchas, de las nieves, y de la falta de alimento".
(Items 7–12 read from page images by reading passes; not re-checked by me. None is optical.)

13. *Gazeta de Madrid* 13 Jun 1809 (GMD-1809-164), from Turin: "Escriben de Cavour, distrito de Pignerol, que el día 30 de abril último se sintió un violento temblor de tierra, que puso á los habitantes en la mayor consternación" (text layer, OCR-only; part of the long Pinerolo earthquake sequence that began 2 Apr 1808; seismic, not volcanic).
14. *Gazeta de Madrid* 13 Apr 1809 (GMD-1809-103), Sweden/Finland: "El excesivo frió y la abundancia de nieves no permiten executar ningunas operaciones militares" (OCR-only; ordinary Nordic winter).
15. *Gazeta de Madrid* 9, 21, 24 Jun 1809: early heat and Danube flood (same French bulletin as Hit 10); 26 Jul 1809: "El calor ha sido excesivo en estos tres dias: el termómetro se ha mantenido constantemente al grado 26" (OCR-only).

Hits 1–6 are the well-known Guadarrama snowstorm of 22 Dec 1808 (Napoleon's crossing) and contemporaneous winter weather; not evidence for or against an aerosol veil.

**Palma weather table** (Réaumur thermometer, barometer in Paris inches/lines, wind; observations are for the day before the issue): transcribed to `../data/mallorca_weather_1808-09_1809-07.csv (301 issues; each reading pass's full notes are in `mallorca_reading_passes/`)`. Caveats: several issues reprint the previous day's table unchanged (3/4, 8/9, 16–19, 24/25 Dec; 5/6, 13/14, 20/21, 22/23 Jan; 1–3 Feb repeat late Jan, 11–14 Feb, 16–21 Feb, 26/27 Feb; 5/6 Mar), so treat repeated rows as missing. Cold spell obs. 20–25 Dec 1808 at 5–6.5 °R with pressure falling from 28p10l to 27p5l; cold snap obs. 16 Jan 1809 (7.5–8.5 °R, N winds). Otherwise mild (7.5–13 °R). The "Atmósfera" column gives wind only, never sky state, so the paper cannot show a veil directly.
